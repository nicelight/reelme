# Semantic pack: finding-adjudication

Co-review is useful when another perspective could resolve consequential
uncertainty. The caller chooses whether to use it, the focus, model, and number
of reviewers. Reuse unchanged evidence when appropriate; unavailable delegation
is not a blocker or reason for repeated launch attempts.

Give a reviewer the target, governing evidence, scope, and question. Ask for
concrete material findings rather than a quota or speculative improvements.
The caller evaluates evidence and owns the verdict; agreement is not a vote.
Record only evidence that helps explain the judgment in the existing report.
No separate gate, artifact, status, or handoff is introduced.
